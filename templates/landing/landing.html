Frequently Asked Questions

How can new residents register?

Residents can sign up directly from the registration portal. After inputting their details and selecting their flat, the account enters a pending status waiting for Admin verification before full access is granted.

How does visitor check-in work?

Security guards log incoming visitors by recording name, mobile number, flat destination, and vehicle details. The entry appears active until the guard marks a check-out timestamp.

Can we pay maintenance dues through the portal?

Yes, residents can click the "Pay Now" option on any unpaid invoice, select their payment mode (Card, UPI, NetBanking), and obtain a generated transaction code upon successful authorization.
